# CS-250-Portfolio
Sprint Review &amp; Retrospective + README questions

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I interpret user needs by gathering requirements, identifying the core problems the user is trying to solve, and asking questions to clarify anything that is unclear. Creating "user stories" helps break those needs into specific actionable tasks that focus on deliver value from the user's perspective. This makes it easier to translate requirements into specific features within the program.


How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I approach developing programs by breaking large problems into smaller, more manageable tasks and tackle them step by step. I like to use an iterative approach, where I would develop, test, get feedback, and improve upon that. For Agile processes, I hope to incorperate Agile processes such as Sprint Planning, Daily Scrums, backlog refinement, and retrospectives to maintain flexibility and continuously improve both the product I'm working on and my workflow, and to be able to put my new Agile skills to use!


What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Being a good team member in software development means communicating clearly and effectively, taking responsibility for your tasks, and being open to collaboration. It involves being accountable fo your work while also supporting your team mates, and being open to feedback and striving for continuous improvement. Strong team work and drive helps ensure that projects stay aligned with your goals and are completed efficiently.
